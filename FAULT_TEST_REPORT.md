## Notes

- **Ordering on catch-up.** Waiting messages are sent URGENT first, then NORMAL, each oldest first (`store.pending_for`). Without this, test 1 would deliver the advisory before the fire report.
- **Fixed during testing.** `test_resend_until_ack` (test 2) sometimes failed under load. The broker recorded a send attempt only after sending, so a fast ACK could arrive first and the attempt was never counted. `Broker.send_to` now records the attempt before sending. The suite then passed three runs in a row.
- **Timers used in the demo** (from `config.py`): retry 5 s, no-response escalation 2 min, not-resolved escalation 3 min after filing, escalation check every 10 s. Real-world values would be longer (about 30 min and 24 h); see the README for the settings.
- **Fixed during testing (sign-in).** Closing a refused connection at once, with the client's next line still unread, made Windows reset it, and the client could lose the `ERR`. The broker now half-closes and drains a refused connection (`Session.refuse`). The gateway could also deadlock when closing a session, by closing its socket file from another thread; fixed in `BrokerSession.close`.
